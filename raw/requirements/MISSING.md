# Programs with no requirement PDF

68 of 71 programs in the 2026-27 catalog publish a requirement
PDF. The three below do not. Each is a joint or dual-degree program whose
curriculum is set outside the standard catalog, so Phase 2 should mark them
`unsupported` (hard rule 5: say plainly that the program cannot be planned
reliably, and do not produce a plan).

| Code | Program | Why there is no PDF |
|---|---|---|
| `EABU` | BBA in Eurasian Business | 2+2 joint degree with Nazarbayev University; two years of residence at each. Publishes no requirement PDF — requirements live on an external page, saved as `_index/EABU_requirements.html`. |
| `WBB` | BBA in World Business | World Bachelor in Business across USC, HKUST and Bocconi. The catalog page links no requirement document at all. |
| `T&M-DDP` | BEng/BSc & BBA Dual Degree Program in Technology & Management | Dual degree program. Out of scope — CLAUDE.md section 7 marks dual degrees unsupported. Its page only links the component majors' PDFs (already downloaded separately) plus both school requirement docs. |
