Curriculum REQUIREMENTS

Subject to Changes and Annual Review

Text Area

This innovative joint university program equips students with localized business acumen and multi-lingual communication skills in English, Kazakh (or Russian), and Putonghua. The curriculum fosters a deep understanding of the dynamic business environment in the region, while enabling graduates to navigate cultural complexities and capitalize on opportunities in these rapidly evolving emerging markets.
